<h2 class="section__hd">About</h2>

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Engineering at Pukyong National
University and currently a research intern in the Computer Vision and
Signal Processing Lab (CVSP Lab) at Pusan National University.

My research focuses on understanding the internal representations and
information-processing mechanisms of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs),
and leveraging these insights to improve inference efficiency and mitigate
hallucinations.

More recently, I have been extending this perspective to Multi-Agent Systems
(MAS), with a focus on analyzing how information is communicated and integrated
across agents and ultimately developing more efficient and reliable multi-agent
reasoning frameworks.

</section>
